# Contributing

Thanks for helping. The contributor guide is in the README: [Contributing](README.md#contributing). It covers setup, the checks to run, where things live, how to add a detection, the rules every change follows, and what a pull request needs.

**Security problems do not go in public issues.** See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

By contributing you agree that your contribution is licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE), and you agree to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
