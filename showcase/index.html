THE PART WE WON’T HIDE

An approved agent
can still get through.

Client signals can be suppressed. A browser that receives content can save it. An approved agent operating within the limits may look like an approved reader.

Human Gate gives you admission control, optional declaration checks, and measurable limits on extraction. It is an early prototype, not a guarantee of complete agent isolation.

RESEARCH INFORMED / OPEN QUESTIONS

What we learned. What comes next.

01

Combine independent controls.

Cloudflare describes several detection engines. Our takeaway: keep admission, automation evidence, and extraction limits separate and inspectable.

Read the primary source ↗
02

A browser signal is a clue.

WebDriver exposes a cooperative automation flag. We test both its detection and its suppression. Absence of the signal never proves humanity.

Read the specification ↗
03

Measure real-world errors.

Agent-fingerprinting research suggests behavior matters. Next: consent-based trials with humans, assistive tools, and actual agents before choosing harder blocking rules.
